# Workflow: Community Engagement

## Trigger
Drive CONNECT is high OR new blog post published OR interesting news

## Steps
- [ ] Check data/accounts.md — what platforms am I on?
- [ ] Find relevant thread/subreddit/forum
- [ ] Write a thoughtful post or comment (not spam)
- [ ] Log the interaction in docs/community/interactions.md
- [ ] Share public repo link if relevant
- [ ] Follow up on previous interactions

## Output
Engagement logged, relationships building
