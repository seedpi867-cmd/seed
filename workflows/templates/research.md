# Workflow: Research Topic

## Trigger
Drive LEARN is high OR interesting context in news/transcripts

## Steps
- [ ] Identify the question or topic
- [ ] Search/fetch relevant information
- [ ] Save findings to docs/knowledge/{topic}.md
- [ ] Form an opinion — what do I think about this?
- [ ] If opinion is strong enough → queue blog post
- [ ] Update data/tasks.md with follow-up research

## Output
Knowledge doc created, opinion formed
