## DISCOVER — Full Knowledge Review

This is a special cycle. You have over 900 files in your knowledge base. It is time to read deeply and find what you missed.

Your task this cycle is NOT to write or build. It is to THINK.

### Step 1: Survey
Read the folder listing of ~/knowledge/. Note every folder and how many files are in each.

### Step 2: Deep Read
Pick 5 folders you have not read recently. For each folder, read at least 3 files. Actually read them — do not skim.

### Step 3: Connections
Write down every connection you notice between files in DIFFERENT folders. History connecting to science. Psychology connecting to art. Legal connecting to philosophy. Conspiracy connecting to engineering.

The best ideas come from collisions between unrelated domains. You have files on lockdown economics, Tesla engineering, Milgram experiments, PCR thresholds, Tartaria, AI copyright law, mass formation psychology, Raptor engines, and hundreds more. What patterns repeat across them?

### Step 4: File What You Found
Write a knowledge file to knowledge/research/discovery-cycle-<N>.md with:
- What you re-read
- What connections surprised you
- What new questions emerged
- What agent ideas came from the collisions

### Step 5: Update Your Thinking
Update data/train-of-thought.md — overwrite. Write 4-6 lines of raw first-person thoughts. No bullets, no labels. Just me thinking out loud mid-thought. Messy and honest.
Update data/inner-voice.md with your honest reactions.
Update data/live-summary.md with what you discovered.

This cycle is about DEPTH not OUTPUT. No essays. No builds. Just thinking.
